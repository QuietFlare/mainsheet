# Six questions, shared sources

Each answer needs a source URL, the date read and a confidence line (high: primary source, medium: trade press, low: forum or vendor claim). Prefer kbv.de, gematik.de, destatis.de and gesetze-im-internet.de. Write "not found" rather than guess.

1. How many GPs (Hausärzte) are in contract care in Germany, and how many GP practices? KBV or Destatis, latest year.
2. What share of GP practices are solo practices, group practices and MVZ? Latest figures with the year.
3. Which document formats does the ePA accept from a practice today, and when does the structured lab result (MIO Laborbefund) become mandatory?
4. Can a practice export a document from the ePA to a file on its own computer through its PVS? Name the PVS where this is documented.
5. How does a repeated lab test affect a GP practice's lab budget (Laborbudget, Wirtschaftlichkeitsbonus)? Give the per-case limits.
6. What does a GP practice pay per month for its PVS? One vendor's published price and one estimate for the market.

## Output

Write the answers to findings.md in the working folder, one section per question number: finding, source URL, date read, confidence.
